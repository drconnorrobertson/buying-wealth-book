AI reinvestment priority reviews by business type

AI reinvestment priority reviews workflows for 20 business types, with practical records, prompts, examples and working checklists.

This collection focuses on one decision: sequence improvements after buying a business. The workflow produces a staged reinvestment plan with review dates. Choose the business context to get its relevant records, worked scenario, review questions and editable checklist.

How this workflow works

Rank projects by the actual constraint they address, dependencies and the evidence available. Separate urgent operating repairs from optional growth work.

A high projected return does not make a project executable with the available team.
