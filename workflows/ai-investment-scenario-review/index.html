AI business investment scenario reviews by business type

AI business investment scenario reviews workflows for 20 business types, with practical records, prompts, examples and working checklists.

This collection focuses on one decision: test the effect of explicit operating assumptions. The workflow produces a scenario table with assumption provenance. Choose the business context to get its relevant records, worked scenario, review questions and editable checklist.

How this workflow works

Change one assumption at a time before combining downside or upside cases. Keep formulas in a spreadsheet and have the finance owner review them.

A scenario is an illustration, not a probability forecast.
