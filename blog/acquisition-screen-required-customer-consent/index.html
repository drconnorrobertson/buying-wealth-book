Check customer consent before relying on revenue in an acquisition

Identify important agreements that need review when ownership or the contracting entity changes.

Worked example

The buyer assumes one large client will remain, but the seller has only shared the invoice history. Ask for the executed agreement and amendments before counting the relationship as transferable.

What to request and document

List key customers and the documents available for each. Have counsel review consent or notice provisions and record who will confirm any required approval before closing.
