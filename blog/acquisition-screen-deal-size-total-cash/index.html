Screen a business acquisition against total cash needed at closing

Compare the buyer’s cash limit with the planned payments, expenses and operating reserve.

Worked example

A fictional buyer sets a $200,000 cash limit. The proposal calls for $140,000 in closing cash, $25,000 in transaction expenses and $45,000 in additional operating reserves. Confirm the assumptions behind the $210,000 total before marking the deal as inside the limit.

What to request and document

Write down each use of cash, the supporting source and when the payment is expected. Identify who owns the operating-reserve estimate and separate amounts that still depend on financing or negotiation.
