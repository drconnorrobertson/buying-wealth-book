Check the lease renewal window before screening in an acquisition

Identify whether the planned operating location is available under the timing assumed in the deal.

Worked example

A store’s lease has eight months remaining, and the seller says a renewal is available. Before relying on that location, request the signed lease, the renewal deadline and any documents about assignment or consent.

What to request and document

Record the terms as written, who can confirm them, and the latest date for resolving them. Ask the buyer’s attorney to review restrictions and keep the location risk open until there is supporting evidence.
