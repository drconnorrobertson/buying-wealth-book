Check the seasonal cash trough in an acquisition screen

Use month-by-month records to identify months that need a larger working-capital cushion.

Worked example

A company reports a profitable year, but a comparison of monthly collections and payments shows a February low point. Ask whether the monthly pattern repeats and what changes if one large customer pays late.

What to request and document

Request monthly cash activity for comparable years and identify abnormal items. Record the low balance, the date expected and which forecast assumptions remain open before using the result in diligence.
