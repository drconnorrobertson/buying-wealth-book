Screen an acquisition for gaps in management coverage

Identify which recurring decisions depend on the seller and who can handle them during the handoff.

Worked example

The seller approves quotes and calls the bank, while the operations manager handles scheduling. Ask for a week of decision records to see whether both seller duties can move to someone else at close.

What to request and document

List approvals and customer relationships by present owner. For each responsibility, name an interim owner, required access and a test that shows the handoff works. Include any management replacement cost in the buyer’s review.
